US state boundaries used to place a people-group point in a state.

Source geometry: US Census Bureau cartographic boundary files, simplified and
redistributed by the us-atlas project (https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas),
file states-10m.json, package version 3.0.1. Census boundary data is in the
public domain.

This GeoJSON was converted from that TopoJSON and limited to the 50 states and
the District of Columbia. US territories were left out on purpose. It is a
boundary file, not a copy of any people-group dataset.
